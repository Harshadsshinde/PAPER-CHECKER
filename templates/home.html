Getting Started 🚀

Important Instructions:

  • Our AI-Based Answer Sheet Evaluation System is an intelligent platform designed to automate the process of assessing handwritten exam papers. The system uses advanced Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Image Processing techniques to analyze, evaluate, and grade students' answer sheets with high accuracy and consistency.
  • Traditional paper-based evaluation is time-consuming, labor-intensive, and prone to human error. Our solution addresses these challenges by offering a fast, transparent, and reliable alternative. By leveraging Optical Character Recognition (OCR) and Machine Learning (ML) models, the system can read handwritten responses, compare them with model answers, and assign fair marks based on content relevance and accuracy.
  • The goal of this project is to assist educational institutions in reducing manual workload, increasing grading efficiency, and ensuring unbiased evaluation. It also provides digital reports and analytics to help teachers and administrators monitor performance trends easily.
  • In short, our system combines AI innovation with academic needs to create a smarter, more efficient examination evaluation process for the future of education.
